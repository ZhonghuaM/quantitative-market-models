# Assumptions and Limitations

- This is a research and portfolio project, not investment advice.
- Backtests are hypothetical and historical. They may not represent live performance.
- Transaction costs are simplified as a basis-point turnover charge.
- The bundled datasets are small samples chosen for reproducibility.
- The default trading model uses simple OHLCV technical features and does not include fundamentals, macro data, or live execution constraints.
- Portfolio examples use stylized assumptions. In real allocation work, expected returns are unstable and constraints matter.
- VaR and Expected Shortfall diagnostics are model-dependent and should be paired with stress testing and judgement.
- Derivatives examples assume constant rates and volatility unless explicitly simulated otherwise.
- Optional external data downloads depend on upstream service availability and terms of use.
